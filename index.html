Afra Wang

writer, journalist, builder · California and London

I write about technological modernity through a humanistic lens, across China and Silicon Valley in the age of AI.

My essays have appeared in Noema, WIRED, the Los Angeles Review of Books, Asterisk, ChinaTalk, Le Grand Continent (in French), and The Ideas Letter, and in the book L'Ennemi qui nous désigne (Gallimard, in French). My work has been quoted or cited in The New York Times, The Guardian, the BBC, and NBC News, and in Patrick McGee's bestselling book Apple in China.

I write the newsletter Concurrent. With three of my best friends, I also co-host CyberPink 疲惫娇娃 (previously known as Loud Murmurs), a Mandarin-language podcast on popular culture, movies, and anything zeitgeisty, now past ninety episodes.

I'm a recipient of Emergent Ventures, and I was a Winter Fellow at GovAI researching the history of open source in China's tech world, and a fellow at the Roots of Progress Institute.

Before writing full-time, I spent six years inside Silicon Valley, running growth for AI news apps and building hacker houses in crypto. I grew up in China until eighteen, then lived in California, New York, and London. I studied the history of technology, international history, and media studies at Columbia, LSE, and UC Irvine. The internet is my actual hometown.

Museums give me highs; mountains keep me honest, whether I'm snowboarding, mountain biking, or hiking. I cook with a beautiful steel wok that I'm unreasonably proud of (yes, you need one too). I read everything, but especially neuroscience, history, and art, and given half a chance I will book-pill you. In return, I'm always taking film recommendations.

Six years inside Silicon Valley, building products and the developer communities around them.

Zero-knowledge infrastructure company on Ethereum. Wrote the go-to-market plan for its ZK proof aggregation product UPA, and built Proof Pass, a ZK ticketing product on NEBRA's stack. Ran our social presence and ecosystem partnerships.

Led the "Proof Day" event series in Brussels, SF, NYC, Denver, and Istanbul: technical days for cryptographers and developers working on zero-knowledge proofs, open-source infrastructure, and privacy tech. Went through a16z's CSX in London and presented at demo day.

Growth Marketing and Operations, Mask Network

Worked on the next-gen social internet: decentralized social protocols, decentralized identity (DIDs), and public goods. Founding growth manager for Firefly, a social app that brings Farcaster, Lens Protocol, and X/Twitter into one feed. Started and hosted the bilingual podcast Web3 Revolution (since shut down).

Built and ran developer communities in person: hacker houses, hackathons, and builder gatherings where people actually ship. ZuSocial, a two-week hacker house; Social Oneness Day and Hackathon; Web3 Social House at ETHDenver 2023; Thinking Beyond the Twitter Box at Zuzalu; DAO Montenegro; Decentralized Social Day at SXSW; The Future of Human Connection at DevCon; and ETH Shanghai 2022 and 2023, including a fireside chat with Jaan Tallinn.

News Associate, SmartNews

AI-driven news app. Worked with lead product managers and ML engineers in a 24/7 newsroom, at the seam between editorial judgment and the recommendation algorithm, alongside former journalists bringing their expertise to tech.

Founding Editor, NewsBreak

AI-powered local news app. Helped build and grow the product with the Silicon Valley growth playbook: growth hacking, retention strategy, push notification engagement, newsletter growth, and user studies. Worked closely with engineers on news ranking and recommendation algorithms, and built local content and election coverage features.
